# Domain scope — Radar / Microwave Precipitation & Weather Measurement (KISTA "MRRG")

Owner-provided scope memo (definition-level; no patent labels).

## What this domain is about
Measuring precipitation and weather with radio / microwave / radar sensing — the "micro radar
rain gauge" family: using electromagnetic waves, beams and signals to detect and quantify
rain, snow and other weather phenomena, and to verify/test such measurements.

## In scope (core)
- Weather radar and microwave sensing of precipitation: rain, snow, precipitation rate,
  storms, wind field / speed / direction, flood and disaster monitoring.
- Radar hardware and methods used for this: dual-polarization radar, bistatic radar,
  frequency-agile / klystron transmitters, microwave/microburst detection, sodar.
- Verification, testing, calibration and simulation of radio/microwave precipitation
  measurement.

## Out of scope
- Weather description, forecasting or data handling with no radio/microwave/radar sensing
  mechanism.
- Radar/microwave systems unrelated to precipitation or weather (e.g. automotive radar,
  communication).
- Mechanical rain gauges with no radar/microwave sensing.

## Note
The domain couples a weather/precipitation target with a radio/microwave/radar sensing
mechanism; both aspects should be present.
