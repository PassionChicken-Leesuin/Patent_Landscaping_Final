# Domain scope — Satellite GNSS / Pseudolite Positioning & Navigation (KISTA "GOCS")

Owner-provided scope memo (definition-level; no patent labels). Grounds the criteria in the
domain owner's understanding of the technology, the way a KISTA analyst would brief it.

## What this domain is about
Positioning, navigation and timing (PNT) technology for and around satellites — especially
GNSS/GPS-based methods, pseudolites (ground-based GPS-like transmitters, "pGPS"), and rover
positioning used together with (geostationary/complex) satellites. The landscape centers on
how satellite and satellite-augmentation signals are used to determine position, correct
orbits/navigation, and sound the atmosphere.

## In scope (core)
- GNSS / GPS signal processing for positioning, timing, and navigation.
- Pseudolites and pseudo-GPS (pGPS) ground transmitters; rover / reference-station positioning.
- Satellite orbit determination, station-keeping, and autonomous space-vehicle navigation
  (e.g. Kalman-filter navigation, argument-of-perigee / longitude control).
- GNSS radio occultation and GNSS-based atmospheric / ionospheric estimation.
- Precise timing and frequency circuits for GNSS-based tracking.
- Optical / RF communication links that serve satellite positioning-and-navigation systems.

## Out of scope
- Generic satellite communications (data broadcast, TDMA/transmission systems) with no
  positioning/navigation purpose.
- Terrestrial mapping or image products that merely use satellite data but do no PNT.
- Consumer GPS gadgets unrelated to satellite/pseudolite positioning technology.

## Note
GPS/GNSS is CENTRAL to this domain, not excluded. "Complex/geostationary satellite" here
refers to the satellite platform context of the PNT technology, not to satellite-bus
hardware or image fusion.
