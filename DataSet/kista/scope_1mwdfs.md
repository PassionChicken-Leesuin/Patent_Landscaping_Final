# Domain scope — Dual/Multi-Frequency Induction Heating for Surface Hardening (KISTA "1MWDFS")

Owner-provided scope memo (definition-level; no patent labels).

## What this domain is about
Induction heating systems and processes that use dual, multi, selectable, or variable
frequency power to perform surface / contour hardening (heat treatment) of metal parts. The
"1 MW dual-frequency system" is high-power induction hardening equipment.

## In scope (core)
- Induction heating power supplies, inverters, coils and control that apply dual- or
  multi-frequency (simultaneous or switched) energy.
- Surface hardening, case hardening, contour hardening and quenching of metal components
  (gears, shafts, roller followers, valve-train parts, gun barrels, workpieces).
- Heat-treatment furnaces / processes where induction + (dual/multi/variable) frequency
  controls the hardened depth or profile.

## Out of scope
- Antennas, dipoles and RF radiators that happen to be "dual-frequency" — this domain is
  induction *heat treatment*, NOT antenna/communication hardware.
- General power electronics with no induction-hardening application.
- Heating unrelated to surface/contour hardening of metals (e.g. cooking, domestic heating).

## Note
"Dual/multi-frequency" refers to the induction-heating excitation used to control hardening
depth, not to radio frequencies.
