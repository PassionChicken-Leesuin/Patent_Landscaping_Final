# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Hydrogen Storage Technology

## 도메인 정의
Hydrogen Storage Technology encompasses inventions that enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable and efficient manner, ensuring its safe containment and release when needed. These technologies must address the challenges of hydrogen's low boiling point and high energy density by mass, optimizing storage volume and weight efficiency, and ensuring compatibility with existing hydrogen infrastructure. The technology may involve mechanical methods such as compression and cryogenic storage, or chemical methods like metal-organic frameworks and hydrogen storage alloys.

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
The scope of analysis for Hydrogen Storage Technology includes patents that specifically address the storage of hydrogen, focusing on methods and systems that ensure stable, efficient, and safe containment and release of hydrogen. This includes mechanical and chemical storage methods, such as compression, cryogenic storage, metal-organic frameworks, and hydrogen storage alloys. Patents that merely use hydrogen for other purposes, such as production or utilization in fuel cells, are outside the scope unless they directly contribute to the storage process.

## 범위 결정 (클러스터별 in/out)

- [IN] **Hydrogen storage alloys** — Hydrogen storage alloys are directly involved in the storage of hydrogen, fulfilling the domain's core purpose of stable and efficient hydrogen containment.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen fuel cells** — Hydrogen fuel cells focus on the utilization of hydrogen for energy production, not on the storage of hydrogen itself.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen generation and recovery** — These processes focus on producing and recovering hydrogen, not on its storage.
- [IN] **Hydrogen storage systems** — These systems are directly related to the storage of hydrogen, addressing the core tasks of the domain.
- [OUT] **Hydrogenation processes** — Hydrogenation processes involve chemical reactions using hydrogen, not the storage of hydrogen.
- [OUT] **Fuel reforming devices** — Fuel reforming devices are involved in extracting hydrogen from other compounds, not in storing hydrogen.
- [IN] **Nickel-hydrogen batteries** — Nickel-hydrogen batteries involve the storage of hydrogen in a form that can be used for energy storage, aligning with the domain's purpose.
- [OUT] **Electrochemical separation** — Electrochemical separation focuses on purifying hydrogen, not on its storage.
- [IN] **Hydrogen occlusion alloy** — Hydrogen occlusion alloys are used for storing hydrogen, directly contributing to the domain's core purpose.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen production catalyst** — Catalysts for hydrogen production are involved in generating hydrogen, not in storing it.
