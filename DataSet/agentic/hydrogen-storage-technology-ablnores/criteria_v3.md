# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Hydrogen Storage Technology

## 도메인 정의
Hydrogen Storage Technology encompasses inventions that enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable and efficient manner, allowing for its safe release when needed, while maximizing storage density and minimizing energy loss during storage and retrieval processes. These technologies must be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure and focus specifically on the storage aspect, rather than production or conversion of hydrogen.

## 도메인 판단 기준 (C)

- **C1.** The invention must enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable form, ensuring that hydrogen can be safely contained over time.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage alloys, corpus: Hydrogen storage materials
- **C2.** The invention must allow for the safe release of hydrogen when needed, ensuring controlled and efficient retrieval of stored hydrogen.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage materials, corpus: Hydrogen recovery systems
- **C3.** The invention must maximize the storage density of hydrogen, aiming for efficient use of space and materials.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage alloys, corpus: Hydrogen storage materials
- **C4.** The invention must ensure minimal energy loss during the storage and retrieval process, optimizing energy efficiency.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage materials, corpus: Hydrogen recovery systems
- **C5.** The invention must be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure, facilitating integration into current systems.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen supply systems

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
The scope of analysis for Hydrogen Storage Technology includes patents that specifically address the storage of hydrogen, focusing on methods and materials that enable stable, efficient, and safe containment and release of hydrogen. It excludes technologies primarily concerned with hydrogen production, conversion, or unrelated applications that merely mention hydrogen.

## 범위 결정 (클러스터별 in/out)

- [IN] **Hydrogen storage alloys** — These patents focus on materials specifically designed for storing hydrogen, aligning with the core purpose of the domain.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen generation systems** — These patents focus on the production of hydrogen rather than its storage, which is outside the domain's scope.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen fuel cells** — Fuel cells are primarily concerned with the conversion of hydrogen into energy, not its storage.
- [IN] **Hydrogen storage materials** — These patents directly address the materials used for storing hydrogen, fitting the domain's core purpose.
- [IN] **Hydrogen recovery systems** — These systems involve the retrieval of stored hydrogen, which is a critical aspect of the storage process.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen gas purification** — Purification processes focus on cleaning hydrogen rather than storing it.
- [IN] **Hydrogen supply systems** — These systems may include storage components as part of the supply chain, relevant to the domain.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen production methods** — These methods focus on generating hydrogen, not storing it.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen separation technologies** — Separation technologies are concerned with isolating hydrogen from other substances, not its storage.

## 제외 기준 (E)

- **E1.** Patents that focus on hydrogen transport without storage are excluded, as they do not perform the storage task.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen supply systems
- **E2.** Patents that focus on hydrogen purification processes rather than storage are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen gas purification
- **E3.** Patents that focus on hydrogen separation technologies rather than storage are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen separation technologies

## 경계 판정 지침

- Cooling tower patents are excluded as they focus on cooling technology, not hydrogen storage.
- Electrochemical reaction apparatus patents are included if they specifically address hydrogen storage, such as by incorporating storage materials or mechanisms, otherwise excluded.
- Gasification of plastics patents are excluded as they focus on decomposition into hydrogen, not storage.
- Device and method for controlling screening size of exhaust sintering gas activated carbon patents are excluded as they pertain to gas cleaning, not hydrogen storage.
