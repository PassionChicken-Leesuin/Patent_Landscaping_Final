# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Autonomous Vehicle Technology

## 도메인 정의
Autonomous Vehicle Technology encompasses systems and methods that enable vehicles to operate independently without human intervention. This includes the ability to navigate roads, detect and respond to dynamic obstacles and traffic signals in real-time, ensure passenger safety through collision avoidance systems, integrate with mapping and GPS systems for route planning and navigation, and comply with legal and regulatory standards for autonomous operation.

## 도메인 판단 기준 (C)

- **C1.** The invention must enable a vehicle to navigate roads and traffic autonomously without human input.
  - 근거: corpus: Autonomous Navigation, corpus: Dynamic Route Planning
- **C2.** The invention must include systems for detecting and responding to dynamic obstacles and traffic signals in real-time.
  - 근거: corpus: Object Detection and Avoidance, corpus: Traffic Sign Recognition
- **C3.** The invention must incorporate collision avoidance systems to ensure passenger safety.
  - 근거: corpus: Collision Avoidance Systems, corpus: Hazard Detection Systems
- **C4.** The invention must integrate with mapping and GPS systems for route planning and navigation.
  - 근거: corpus: Vehicle Navigation Systems, corpus: High Definition Map Updating
- **C5.** The invention must include features that suggest compliance with safety or operational protocols aligning with known standards.
  - 근거: web: Automated vehicles in EU legislation refer specifically to road vehicles like cars, trucks, or buses.
- **C6.** The invention must actively use V2X communication systems for interaction with the environment and other vehicles to perform autonomous operation tasks.
  - 근거: corpus: Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) Communication, corpus: V2V Communication
- **C7.** The invention must actively use sensor integration and cross-validation to ensure system reliability and accuracy for autonomous operation tasks.
  - 근거: corpus: Sensor Cross-Validation, corpus: Sensor Integration for Autonomous Driving

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
The scope of analysis for Autonomous Vehicle Technology includes patents that describe systems and methods enabling vehicles to operate independently without human intervention. This encompasses technologies for autonomous navigation, obstacle detection and avoidance, collision prevention, integration with mapping and GPS systems, and compliance with regulatory standards. Patents that focus on enabling technologies, such as sensor integration and communication systems, are also included if they directly support autonomous vehicle operation.

- **E1.** Patents that focus on systems providing alerts without control, such as lane departure warnings without corrective action, are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Lane departure warning systems are designed to warn drivers when a vehicle begins to move out of its lane.
- **E2.** Patents related to unmanned aerial vehicles or marine vehicles are excluded as they do not pertain to land-based autonomous vehicles.
  - 근거: corpus: Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, corpus: Unmanned ship autopilot

  - 근거: corpus: Gate opening and closing system
