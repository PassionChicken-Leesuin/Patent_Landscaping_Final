# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Hydrogen Storage Technology

## 도메인 정의
Hydrogen Storage Technology encompasses inventions that enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable and efficient manner, ensuring its safe containment and release when needed. These technologies must optimize the volume and weight efficiency of storage systems and be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure, facilitating the use of hydrogen as an energy carrier.

## 도메인 판단 기준 (C)

- **C1.** The invention must enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable form, such as through compression, liquefaction, or chemical bonding.
  - 근거: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_storage, https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-storage
- **C2.** The invention must allow for the efficient release of hydrogen when needed, ensuring that the stored hydrogen can be utilized effectively.
  - 근거: https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-storage, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360319919310025
- **C3.** The invention must ensure the safety of hydrogen storage under various conditions, including temperature and pressure variations.
  - 근거: https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-storage, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360319919310025
- **C4.** The invention must optimize the volume and weight efficiency of hydrogen storage, making it feasible for applications such as transportation.
  - 근거: https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-storage, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360319919310025
- **C5.** The invention must be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure, facilitating integration into current systems.
  - 근거: https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-storage, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360319919310025

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
The scope of analysis for Hydrogen Storage Technology includes patents that specifically address the methods and systems for storing hydrogen in a stable, efficient, and safe manner. This includes technologies that involve mechanical, chemical, or novel material approaches to hydrogen storage, as well as those that ensure compatibility with existing hydrogen infrastructure. Patents that focus solely on the production or utilization of hydrogen, without addressing storage, are outside the scope.

## 범위 결정 (클러스터별 in/out)

- [IN] **Chemical storage methods** — Chemical storage methods are included as they directly address the core purpose of storing hydrogen in a stable and efficient manner.
- [IN] **Mechanical storage methods** — Mechanical storage methods are included as they are fundamental techniques for achieving stable and efficient hydrogen storage.
- [IN] **Novel materials for hydrogen storage** — Novel materials, such as metal-organic frameworks, are included as they represent advanced approaches to enhancing hydrogen storage capabilities.

## 제외 기준 (E)

- **E1.** Patents that focus solely on the production of hydrogen, such as electrolysis or steam methane reforming, without addressing storage, are excluded.
  - 근거: https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-production
- **E2.** Patents that focus solely on the utilization of hydrogen, such as fuel cells or combustion engines, without addressing storage, are excluded.
  - 근거: https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-use
- **E3.** Patents that focus on hydrogen transportation or conversion processes without addressing the storage aspect as a primary function are excluded.
  - 근거: https://www.energy.gov/eere/fuelcells/hydrogen-use

## 경계 판정 지침

- For cases like hydrogenation of CO2 to methanol, the judge should determine if the primary function is hydrogen storage or if storage is incidental to another process. If storage is a secondary function, it should be excluded. Consider whether the patent explicitly claims improvements or innovations in the storage process itself.
