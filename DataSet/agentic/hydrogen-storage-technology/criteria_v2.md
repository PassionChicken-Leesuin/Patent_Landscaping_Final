# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Hydrogen Storage Technology

## 도메인 정의
Hydrogen Storage Technology involves inventions that actively store hydrogen in a stable and safe manner, allowing for its efficient and controlled release when needed. These technologies must optimize the volume and weight efficiency of the storage system and be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure. Unique aspects include the use of specific storage mechanisms such as metal hydrides or hydrogen storage alloys, and ensuring safety under various environmental conditions.

## 도메인 판단 기준 (C)

- **C1.** The invention must actively store hydrogen in a stable form using specific mechanisms such as metal hydrides or hydrogen storage alloys.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage alloys, corpus: Metal hydride tanks
- **C2.** The invention must actively facilitate the controlled release of hydrogen under specified conditions, indicating a mechanism for controlled hydrogen release.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen release, corpus: Hydrogen storage systems
- **C3.** The invention must ensure the safety of hydrogen storage under various environmental conditions, including temperature and pressure variations.
  - 근거: corpus: Comprehensive hydrogen storage systems
- **C4.** The invention must optimize the volume and weight efficiency of hydrogen storage, potentially through advanced materials or design innovations.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage alloys for improved efficiency
- **C5.** The invention must be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure, such as integration with fuel cells or hydrogen refueling stations.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen fuel cells, corpus: Hydrogen separation and storage

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
Patents within the scope of Hydrogen Storage Technology include those that specifically address the active storage of hydrogen in a stable, safe, and efficient manner, with mechanisms for controlled release and compatibility with existing hydrogen infrastructure. This includes technologies involving metal hydrides, hydrogen storage alloys, and comprehensive storage systems.

## 제외 기준 (E)

- **E1.** Technologies focused on hydrogen production without storage capabilities are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen production methods
- **E2.** Technologies related to hydrogen purification that do not involve storage mechanisms are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen purification methods
- **E3.** Technologies involving hydrogen in processes unrelated to storage, such as crude oil treatment or plastic gasification, are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Process for the treatment of crude oil using hydrogen, corpus: Gasification of plastics
- **E4.** Technologies related to hydrogen fuel cells that do not address storage mechanisms are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Advanced hydrogen fuel cell systems

## 경계 판정 지침

- Patents that mention hydrogen storage but focus primarily on unrelated chemical processes should be excluded unless they demonstrate a clear mechanism for hydrogen storage.
- Patents involving electrochemical reactions but do not specifically address hydrogen storage should be excluded, as seen in the electrochemical reaction apparatus cases.
- Patents focusing on cooling technologies or cable protection, which do not relate to hydrogen storage, should be excluded.
