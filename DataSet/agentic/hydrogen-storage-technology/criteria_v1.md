# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Hydrogen Storage Technology

## 도메인 정의
Hydrogen Storage Technology encompasses inventions that enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable and safe manner, allowing for its efficient release when needed, while optimizing the volume and weight efficiency of the storage system. These technologies must be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure and ensure safety under various conditions.

## 도메인 판단 기준 (C)

- **C1.** The invention must enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable form, such as through the use of metal hydrides or hydrogen storage alloys.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage alloys, corpus: Metal hydride tanks
- **C2.** The invention must facilitate the efficient release of hydrogen when required, indicating a mechanism for controlled hydrogen release.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen release, corpus: Hydrogen storage systems
- **C3.** The invention must ensure the safety of hydrogen storage under various environmental conditions, including temperature and pressure variations.
  - 근거: corpus: Comprehensive hydrogen storage systems
- **C4.** The invention must optimize the volume and weight efficiency of hydrogen storage, potentially through advanced materials or design innovations.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen storage alloys for improved efficiency
- **C5.** The invention must be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure, such as integration with fuel cells or hydrogen refueling stations.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen fuel cells, corpus: Hydrogen separation and storage

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
Patents within the scope of Hydrogen Storage Technology include those that specifically address the storage of hydrogen in a stable, safe, and efficient manner, with mechanisms for controlled release and compatibility with existing hydrogen infrastructure. This includes technologies involving metal hydrides, hydrogen storage alloys, and comprehensive storage systems.

## 제외 기준 (E)

- **E1.** Patents focused solely on hydrogen production methods without addressing storage are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen production methods
- **E2.** Patents related to hydrogen purification methods that do not involve storage are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Hydrogen purification methods
- **E3.** Patents on hydrogen fuel cells that do not address storage mechanisms are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Advanced hydrogen fuel cell systems
- **E4.** Patents involving hydrogen in processes unrelated to storage, such as crude oil treatment or plastic gasification, are excluded.
  - 근거: corpus: Process for the treatment of crude oil using hydrogen, corpus: Gasification of plastics

## 경계 판정 지침

- Patents mentioning hydrogen gas in the context of unrelated chemical processes, such as polyamide production or tungsten powder preparation, should be excluded as they do not address hydrogen storage.
- Patents that involve electrochemical reactions but do not specifically address hydrogen storage should be excluded, as seen in the electrochemical reaction apparatus cases.
- Patents focusing on cooling technologies or cable protection, which do not relate to hydrogen storage, should be excluded.
