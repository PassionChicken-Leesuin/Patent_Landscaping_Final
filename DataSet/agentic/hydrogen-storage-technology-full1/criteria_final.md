# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Hydrogen Storage Technology

## 도메인 정의
Hydrogen Storage Technology encompasses inventions that enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable and efficient manner, ensuring its safe containment and release when needed. These technologies must optimize the volume and weight efficiency of storage systems and be compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure. The core function is to store hydrogen in a form that allows for its practical use in various applications, such as transportation and energy systems, without compromising safety or efficiency.

## 도메인 판단 기준 (C)

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
The scope of analysis for Hydrogen Storage Technology includes patents that focus on the methods and materials for storing hydrogen in a stable, efficient, and safe manner. This includes technologies that address the physical and chemical storage of hydrogen, as well as systems that ensure the practical release and use of stored hydrogen. Patents that focus solely on hydrogen production, utilization, or unrelated applications are outside the scope.

## 범위 결정 (클러스터별 in/out)

- [IN] **Hydrogen storage materials** — These patents focus on materials specifically designed for storing hydrogen, aligning with the core purpose of the domain.
- [IN] **Hydrogen storage alloys** — Alloys designed for hydrogen storage are directly relevant to the domain as they provide a means to store hydrogen efficiently.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen generation systems** — These systems focus on producing hydrogen rather than storing it, which is outside the core purpose of the domain.
- [OUT] **Hydrogen production methods** — Methods for producing hydrogen do not address the storage aspect, which is the focus of this domain.
- [OUT] **Fuel cell technologies** — Fuel cells are primarily concerned with the utilization of hydrogen, not its storage.
- [IN] **Hydrogen storage systems** — These systems are directly involved in the storage of hydrogen, fitting the domain's core purpose.

- Patents on safety during refueling, such as 'Driver interactive system for reducing the possibility of a static discharge during the refill of high pressure storage tanks in hydrogen fuel cell powered vehicles', are excluded as they focus on refueling safety rather than storage technology.
- Patents on hydrogen detection, like 'Hydrogen gas sensor', are excluded as they do not address the storage of hydrogen.
- Patents involving advanced materials like MOFs should be included if they enhance hydrogen storage capabilities, even if they are underrepresented in the patent pool.
- Patents that involve both storage and utilization, such as integrated systems that store and release hydrogen for immediate use, should be evaluated for their primary focus on storage. If the primary function is storage, they should be included.
