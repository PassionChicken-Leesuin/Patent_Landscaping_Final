# 도메인 판단 기준서 — Hydrogen Storage Technology

## 도메인 정의
Hydrogen Storage Technology encompasses inventions that enable the storage of hydrogen in a stable and efficient manner, ensuring its safe containment and controlled release for use in various applications. This includes methods and systems that optimize the volume and weight efficiency of hydrogen storage, maintain safety under different conditions, and are compatible with existing hydrogen infrastructure. The technology must specifically address the challenges of storing hydrogen in gaseous, liquid, or solid forms, and facilitate its retrieval for energy conversion or other uses.

## 분석 대상 특허의 범위
The scope of analysis for Hydrogen Storage Technology includes patents that specifically address the storage of hydrogen in various forms, focusing on stability, efficiency, safety, and compatibility with existing infrastructure. It excludes technologies that merely use hydrogen for other purposes without addressing its storage challenges.

- [OUT] **Hydrogen generation and recovery** — These processes focus on producing hydrogen, not on storing it.
